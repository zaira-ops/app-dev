## ⭐ Overview
**Titanic** is a 1997 American epic romance and disaster film directed, written, and co-produced by **James Cameron**. It stars:

- Leonardo DiCaprio as **Jack Dawson**
- Kate Winslet as **Rose DeWitt Bukater**

Set against the ill-fated maiden voyage of the **RMS Titanic**, the film blends historical and fictionalized elements to tell a heart-wrenching love story.

---

## 🛳️ Plot Highlights

1. Jack wins a ticket aboard the Titanic in a poker game.
2. Rose feels trapped in her upper-class life and meets Jack.
3. They fall in love as the ship sails.
4. Disaster strikes — the Titanic hits an iceberg.
5. Jack sacrifices himself so Rose can survive.

---
